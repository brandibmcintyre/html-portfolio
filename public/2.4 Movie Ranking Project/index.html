The Three Best Movies According to Brandi

My top three movies of all time.

JAWS

Man vs. beast. A story as old as time. Unforgetable characters and a location that feels me with nostalgia.

The Shining

If you love a good scare, be it from ghosts or the human psyche, this is the movie for you.

Ghostbusters

Comforting and funny. Only Bill Murray could make chasing ghosts in New York City a comedic pastime.
